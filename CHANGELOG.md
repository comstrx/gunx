# Changelog

Every release of GunX: one section per tag, one block per tool, short bullets.

## v0.1.0

### rust

- The workspace, the lint wall and the complete proof chain.
- `hello`, the first domain, behind the `engine` facade.

### go

- The workspace: one module per domain and an import guard.
- `hello`, the first domain, with its recorded public surface.

### python

- Fifty domains behind one `engine` namespace.
- One fault, typed settings and an adapter family for every provider.
- A test, a benchmark and a fuzz target for every domain.

### typescript

- Thirty-seven members behind one `engine` facade, portable across web, mobile and desktop.
- `spec` and `tool`: surfaces declared as data and built by one runner.

### bash

- The gates of Rust, Go, Python, Bun and Bash behind one command.
- `extract`: a part of a repository into its own, proven before it is delivered.
- One self-contained bundle with `install`, `update` and `remove`.

### apex-api

- Five members: `contract`, `gateway`, `iam`, `notify` and `hello`.
- One layout and one ladder of layers, enforced by laws in the suite.

### apex-web

- Web and desktop surfaces from one tree of role specs.
- A byte budget on every build.

### apex-mobile

- Native roles from specs on Expo, behind one bridge to native capabilities.
- A byte budget on the Hermes bundle.
