# Vision

## Contract

An engineering umbrella develops reusable general engines and independent products above them. Its lasting value is accumulated engineering capability.

## Structure

Real need → identify responsibility → develop a general capability → establish its behavior through tests and use → benefit other consumers.
Tree owns the structural vocabulary; architecture owns the dependency and responsibility laws.

## Rules

### Engineering ambition

- All engines have equal engineering importance and the same uncompromising quality bar.
- Every engine pursues the greatest practical speed, safety, memory efficiency, capability breadth and clarity of logic.
- Each exposes a powerful, coherent DSL that people can predict and use easily. Simplicity must preserve capability, quality, speed and safety.
- Adapt methods to each ecosystem; no engine is a disposable prototype, subordinate implementation or excuse for incomplete work.
- Training means engineering improvement, not automatic machine learning or data sharing between products.
- An approved foundation round may capture accumulated experience. Product complexity stays with its business owner; thin layers are an outcome, not a line-count target.
- Claims about performance and safety describe observed facts; ambition does not substitute for them.

### Distribution

- An engine can be extracted into an independent tool and repository; reusable product-part sources can also be distributed.
- The laboratory product supplies reusable part implementations, including their specifications. The actual source product and selected API language belong to its product knowledge.
- Target names, repositories, source paths and distribution timing are configuration data, not fixed names taught here.
- Keep one authoritative development source for each capability. An extracted release destination is not a competing development source.
- Shared mechanisms support local extraction and remote distribution, with target-specific transformations expressed as data.
- One version coordinates the repository; distribution moments may be tag, release or manual as configured.
- Distribution and production activation remain distinct actions with their own intent and authorization.
- A directory's existence does not approve a product roadmap, migration, delivery date or additional distribution target.

## Proof

Every engine is held to the same standard; reusable mechanisms remain product-independent and each consumer retains its business decisions.
