# Root

## Contract

Universal governance, studied before specialized knowledge. No product or implementation selection lives here.

## Structure

Each child owns one concern. Work references commands, communication and history; their entries own their protocols.

## Rules

Complete every foundation listed in Routing before selecting onward work or asking about scope.

Scope may be one unit, a selected set, all engines, all products, or the whole approved knowledge tree.
"Whole project" or "all approved knowledge" visits all stack branches and the mcp, ipc and history writing guides through their entries; it never authorizes reading historical contents or external memories.
Root/work owns default roles, scope authority and access limits; apply that contract when selecting the next activity.

## Proof

All mandatory foundations studied. Intended next entry and reason recorded. No action invented.

## Sources

Owner decisions govern; each technical child cites its evidence.
