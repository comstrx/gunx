# Apex business

## Contract

Own business facts and open decisions. This is an enterprise laboratory, not a license to invent a commercial roadmap.

## Structure

Approved high-level intent: multi-tenant, multi-role, multi-product-type SaaS demonstrating the whole platform lifecycle.
Implementation status is observed in the assigned source, not inferred from this brief.
Actors, workflows and commercial scope require their own approved brief; inherited examples do not establish those decisions.

## Rules

- Keep tenant organizations, membership, roles and permissions explicit; tenancy and authorization are business contracts enforced through shared mechanisms.
- Specify who owns each business record, decision and state transition; technical service organization belongs to the API part.
- Financial amounts, status changes, audit attribution and side effects need explicit business invariants and failure behavior.
- A requested feature needs actors, scope, inputs, outputs, permissions, states and acceptance criteria; ask only for missing material decisions.
- Providers and commercial integrations are not selected just because the engine supports them.
- Do not infer hosts, pricing, settlement rules, service list, legal/compliance claims or customer commitments.
- A demonstration or placeholder is not an approved commercial workflow.

## Proof

Separate approved requirements, source observations and open decisions. No engine skill absorbs Apex's policy.
