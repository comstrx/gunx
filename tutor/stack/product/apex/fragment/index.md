# Apex fragments

## Contract

Select the product-specific part wrapper; do not own general part architecture.

## Structure

Brand and business have already been studied through the product entry; don't repeat their contents here.

## Rules

Infer the part from a concrete task. Ask when the target remains materially ambiguous.
A wrapper records only actual additional choices. General part architecture is inherited through its route.
Selection values are product choices, not language/library lessons. Additional overrides need an approved product-specific reason.
Read the selected part's current manifest and product requirements when applying preferences; a conflict needs a decision rather than an invented replacement.

## Proof

All requested parts, no unrelated parts; each route returns before the next branch begins.

## Sources

Product manifests and the owner-selected scope.

## Routing

| Part   | Child                     |

| infra  | [infra](infra/index.md)   |

Resolve requested parts from the assignment and actual product composition.
A selected part with a registered customization follows its child; other applicable parts enter the [general part dispatcher](../../../part/index.md) without inventing a wrapper.
A full-product task covers all its applicable parts, not only this customization table.
A registered but missing child is a defect, not permission to silently skip preferences.
Each child continues to the general part dispatcher carrying only its additional choices.
