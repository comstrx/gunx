# Apex brand

## Contract

Own Apex identity and presentation preferences; no reusable rendering mechanics.

## Structure

The owner has not approved final palette, logo, typography, illustrations or brand narrative.
Current source assets are implementation evidence, not automatic approval of a permanent identity.

## Rules

- Arabic and English; RTL and LTR; responsive, accessible surfaces.
- Light and dark are distinct art directions sharing one identity.
- Product-owned identity choices apply across its surfaces; shared rendering and accessibility practice belongs to general part knowledge.
- A temporary visual choice is labeled provisional. Do not invent approved branding or brand-dependent business.
- Use original or appropriately licensed assets; retain source/license information without embedding unrelated commercial identity.

## Proof

A brand decision traces to the owner; cross-surface identity stays coherent without moving Apex tokens into an engine.

## Sources

Owner-approved preferences; actual assets and spec contracts verified when a design task starts.
