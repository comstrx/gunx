# Products

## Contract

Register product knowledge, not the company roadmap.

## Structure

Each product owns brand and business. A fragment dispatcher and its children exist only when product-specific part preferences are needed.
An intentionally absent customization is valid; a registered missing entry remains a defect.

## Rules

Unregistered product work requires its brief/knowledge entry; do not reuse another product's business by assumption.
Do not read unrelated customer information to answer a shared-engine question.

## Proof

Selected product matches owner scope and has its own business; optional preferences refine general parts.

## Sources

Owner-approved product registry.
